About

Started with Needs!n Sudan, an attempt to share Sudan people's needs, who have been facing a disastrous war. Needs!n (NeedsIn) is a non-profit platform that aims to spread the information about the needs of the people in places that are affected by wars, conflicts, or disasters. In such circumstances, in addition to their urgent needs, and for various reasons, people also face the difficulty of not being able to reach out to those who can help, and those who can help find it difficult to get updated information. Therefore, emerged the idea for this platform, to help the people in need, by sharing and spreading the information, whether about healthcare, humanitarian, or energy needs.
